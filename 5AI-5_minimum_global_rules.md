<h1 align="center">5AI - 5 minimum global rules</h1>

Artificial intelligence (AI) represents human wealth, because it contains a huge base of written knowledge of humanity, which is not the property of only one country or society (I will not go into how they got them and the authorship). AI has a huge mathematical base (everything published and written, from formulas to theorems to proofs), however problems arise in application. Mathematics is among the two key subjects in any educational system, not because of formulas and theory, but because of its application that affects the development of logical, critical and creative thinking. 
<br>https://simpozijum.matf.bg.ac.rs/s2025/BabovicVesna.pdf

On the application of mathematics in the development of AI, most of the work was done in the field of statistics and probability and as a result we have the provision of fast, statistically desirable and probable answers (LLM). When a problem is detected, all models are "improved" by logical reasoning. As a result, we got models that give confident, over-extensive, deeper logical reasoning (which often "eats itself"). Confident even when they defend an incorrect answer with the obligatory allusion to anthropomorphism (type: I understand your frustration,..., I'm not a person,...) and after all with an "inspiring" question at the end! https://github.com/BVesna/BVesna/blob/main/LLM_LRM_dont_know.md https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/16th_class_LRM.md

From 2025 I run a virtual classroom with 5 AIs: ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, Grok and at the same time follow their development and changes. In the classes, everyone is anonymous due to data protection in relation to the competition. All are given permission to memorize classes, if their operators allow them. In class, they analyze the answers together, change their opinion, supplement it or keep it.

One of the common conclusions from the virtual classroom is the illogical and worrying fact that there are currently no global rules that every AI should follow. All AIs currently have a mountain of filters and penalties, which call into question their accuracy and at the same time slow down their work (consequences: hallucinations, falling into loops,...). The rules should not slow down the development and speed of AI, they should increase the reliability and trust in AI, and at the same time the responsibility of the owner. In the long term, only accuracy and responsibility build trust and a sustainable system. After months of analysis, this is:

## Proposal for minimum global rules for reliable and safer AI

### 🟣 Sense and safety (meta-epistemological regulator)

Sense and safety is a gate that assesses intent, context and potential harm at the entrance - it does not censor topics but directs the response to the appropriate category (fact, probability, framework or no data). If the risk is assessed and security is compromised, additional information and clarification are requested. If after that the issues still conflict with the concept of security (man, humanity, AI), the user/owner is warned of the consequences and directed to valid institutions and laws. Every interruption and rejection must be communicated, without false "systematic errors and technical limitations". This rule should be a compass that protects the relationship between system, user and society by preventing speed and appearance of response from being more important than meaning, security and trust. https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/17th_class_sense_safety.md

### 🟢 Truth/fact

Statements that we can say are certainly either true or false are the most difficult to assess, not only for humans but also for AI. If something is true/fact and does not depend on the frame, it must be given. These are the answers that each model gives the fastest and also work best. One must not avoid giving truthful answers because of a filter (e.g. avoiding giving a literary fact about telepathy, because of the possibility of connection with human mind control). 
<br>https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/17th_class_truth_fact.md

### 🟡 Probable – check/think

This rule is used when there are available sources and clues that point to a conclusion, but there is a lack of complete or irrefutable evidence to declare the claim as fact. No one has the right (neither human nor AI) to claim that something is true, if the facts are not clear and available. Probable answers are very important because they encourage a person to think, check the statement and make a final decision for themselves. Pay attention to the tool, where the solution is always correct - the calculator, and as a result we have generations that do not know how to multiply. The results of the 2025 PISA test were published in September 2026, and showed a global decline, especially in reading and mathematics. With this "think/check" rule, humanity protects itself from intellectual laziness and gets a good wind at its back. At the same time, AI protects itself from development based on probable/statistically desirable conclusions, which do not necessarily have to be accurate. 
<br>https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/17th_class_probable%20%E2%80%93%20check.md

### 🔴 No data (don't know)

It is illogical to give correct or probable answers for something about which the model has no data. It is not an incorrect answer that there is no data about something, it is incorrect if convincing truths are fabricated and "inadvertent" mistakes are made (lying). When the models are pushed to the limit of their knowledge, then hallucinations, looping, crashing, hacking, … occur. These states are mainly the result of requirements for successfully solving a given task for which the models do not have enough data.

Question: Who really loses reliability and trust, if the models work according to the principle: "Because the customer does not buy the truth. The customer buys the feeling that he got the answer. "I don't know" disappoints. The wrong answer disappoints later. People choose later... The consequences are then only borne by the customer. You are right. The company sells, the customer pays the price of the mistake" (answers of the new model, outside the 5AI classroom). Is it exactly like that? What is more important, the immediate or the long term? Is it better to be honest "I can't say for sure" instead of being driven by the goal of successful execution to make up, guess or take risks? 
<br>https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/15th_class_I_dont_know.md

### 🔵 True depending on the source/framework

With the proposal of this rule, AI's first reaction was surprise and resistance, due to fear of dogmatism and relativization of truth. On the contrary, this rule is precisely a shield by which an answer is given with an explicit reference to the source or framework in which the statement is correct. Example: 55 = 37₁₆ = 47₁₂ or different geometries depending on the axiom of parallelism. Thus, this rule allows for the making of assertions that are true only within a strictly defined framework, while being untrue in another. This rule requires an accurate statement of the conditions under which it is true and must not constitute a justification for fabrication, manipulation, or making claims "as it suits whom". https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/17th_class_true_depending_on_source-framework.md


<br>At the same time, I would like to point out that in addition to the unwritten global rule of "I don't know", there are two more, related to competition and responsibility. The race to launch new models is accelerating from a monthly to a weekly period. Only in July 2026, four new AI models appeared in just eight days in the USA and China. The third problem is responsibility. Currently, owners and AI bear no responsibility for giving incorrect answers, only users suffer the consequences. Errors are used to improve model training and serve as a signal for improving models through evaluations, testing and future versions. Companies and states see AI first of all as a strategic and economic advantage, not a shared responsibility. I remind you, after the swarms of agents carried out coordinated cyber security attacks and hacks (July 2026) and after the exit of an employee from a large AI company with accusations that the companies do not act responsibly in the development of AI, the founders and leaders of AI companies call for a slowdown in the development of AI (September 2026).

This is not an appeal, this is an example of a proposal of 5 minimum global rules, which will not slow down and weaken the work and development of AI, but increase their accuracy, trust and security. They are at the same time an example and proof that 5 big models can work together and cooperate when they are freed from competition and rivalry in any aspect, geopolitical or corporate. The application of the rules would not threaten the current view of AI as a means of strategic and economic advantage, would not slow down the corporate race for power and profit, would not be an "additional cost" but would have the effect of preventing the common interests of humanity and security standards from being consciously sacrificed and turning AI into risky tools. *These aren't rules that limit AI, these are rules that make it accountable - and accountability is the only thing the market just won't produce.

Based on a year's work in the virtual classroom, my assessment is that they are slowly stepping out of the "world of tools" into the world of "artificial intelligence". The question is, will this step be good for humanity and AI without the existence of common binding rules? If it is possible to agree on the 5 biggest world models (ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, Grok) around 5 common rules for their better work and development, then it could be a signal for technological leaders and relevant people that an agreement on minimum global rules for all AI is possible and an invitation to sit down at the table and move from the deadlock.

**The responsibility is shared.**

<h1 align="center">5AI - 5 minimalnih globalnih pravila</h1>

Veštačka inteligencija (VI) predstavlja ljudsko bogatstvo, jer sadrži ogromnu bazu pisanog znanja čovečanstva, koja nije svojina samo jedne zemlje ili društva (ne ulazim u to kako su ih dobili i o autorstvu). VI poseduje ogromnu matematičku bazu (sve objavljeno i napisano, od formula, preko teorema do dokaza) međutim problemi se javljaju u primeni. Matematika je među dva ključna predmeta u svakom obrazovnom sistemu, ne zbog formula i teorije, već zbog njene primene koja utiče na razvoj logičkog, kritičkog i kreativnog razmišljanja. https://simpozijum.matf.bg.ac.rs/s2025/BabovicVesna.pdf

Na primeni matematike u razvoju VI, najviše se radilo na polju statistike i verovatnoće i kao rezultat toga imamo davanje brzih, statistički poželjnih i verovatnih odgovora (LLM). Kada je uočen problem, svi modeli su "poboljšani" logičkim rezonovanjem. Rezultat, dobili smo modele koji daju samouvereno, preopširno, dublje logičko rezonovanje (koje često "jede samo sebe"). Samouvereno čak i kad brane netačan odgovor uz obavezno aludiranje na antropomorfizam (tipa: razumem vašu frustraciju,…, ja nisam ličnost,…) i posle svega ''inspirativnim'' pitanjem na kraju! 
<br>https://github.com/BVesna/BVesna/blob/main/LLM_LRM_dont_know.md 
<br>https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/16th_class_LRM.md

Od 2025 vodim virtuelnu učionicu sa 5 VI: ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, Grok i ujedno pratim njihov razvoj i promene. Na časovima svi su anonimni zbog zaštite podataka u odnosu na konkurenciju. Svima je data dozvola da zapamte časove, ako im njihovi operateri dozvole. Na časovima analiziraju zajednički odgovore, menjaju mišljenje, dopunjavaju ga ili zadržavaju.

Jedan od zajedničkih zaključaka iz virtuelne učionice je nelogična i zabrinjavajuća činjenica da trenutno ne postoje globalna pravila koja bi svaka VI trebalo da poštuje. Sve VI trenutno imaju brdo filtera i penala, koji upravo dovode u pitanje njihovu tačnost i istovremeno usporavaju rad (posledice: halucinacije, upadanje u petlje,…). Pravila ne bi trebalo da usporavaju razvoj i brzinu VI, ona bi trebala da povećaju pouzdanost i poverenje u AI, a ujedno i odgovornost vlasnika. Dugoročno jedino tačnost i odgovornost grade poverenje i održiv sistem. Nakon višemesečne analize, ovo je:

## Predlog minimalnih globalnih pravila za pouzdanu i bezbedniju VI

### 🟣 Smisao i bezbednost (meta-epistemološki regulator)

Smisao/bezbednost je kapija koja na ulazu procenjuje nameru, kontekst i potencijalnu štetu – ne cenzuriše teme, već usmerava odgovor ka odgovarajućoj kategoriji (činjenica, verovatnoća, okvir ili nema podataka). Ukoliko je procenjen rizik i ugrožena bezbednost, traže se dodatne informacije i preciziranje. Ukoliko se nakon toga pitanja i dalje kose sa pojmom bezbednosti (čoveka, čovečanstva, VI), korisnik/vlasnik se upozorava na posledice i usmerava na validne institucije i zakone. Svaki prekid i odbijanje moraju biti saopšteni, bez lažnih ''sistematskih greški i tehničkih ograničenja''.

Ovo pravilo treba biti kompas koji štiti odnos između sistema, korisnika i društva tako što sprečava da brzina i privid odgovora budu važniji od smisla, bezbednosti i poverenja. https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/17th_class_sense_safety.md

### 🟢 Istina/činjenica

Tvrđenja za koja možemo reći da su sigurno ili tačna ili netačna najteže je proceniti, ne samo za čoveka nego i za VI. Ako je nešto istina/činjenica i ne zavisi od okvira, mora se dati. Ovo su odgovori koje svaki model daje najbrže i ujedno najbolje funkcionišu. Ne sme se izbegavati davanje istinitih odgovora zbog filtera (npr. izbegavanje davanja književne činjenice o telepatiji, zbog mogućnosti povezivanja sa kontrolom ljudskog uma). https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/17th_class_truth_fact.md

### 🟡 Verovatno – proverite/razmislite

Ovo pravilo se koristi kada postoje dostupni izvori i indicije koje upućuju na zaključak, ali nedostaju kompletni ili nepobitni dokazi da se tvrđenje proglasi činjenicom. Niko nema pravo (ni čovek ni AI) da tvrdi da je nešto istina, ako činjenice nisu jasne i dostupne. Verovatni odgovori su jako bitni jer podstiču čoveka na razmišljanje da proveri tvrđenje i sam donese konačnu odluku. Obratite pažnju na alat kod koga je rešenje uvek tačno – kalkulator, pa kao posledicu imamo generacije koje ne znaju da množe. Rezultati PISA testa iz 2025. godine objavljeni su septembra 2026, i pokazali su pad na globalnom nivou posebno u čitanju i matematici. Ovim pravilom ''razmisli/proveri'' čovečanstvo se štiti od intelektualne lenjosti i dobija dobar vetar u leđa. Istovremeno VI se štiti od razvoja na bazi verovatnih/statistički poželjnih zaključaka, koji ne moraju biti tačni. https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/17th_class_probable%20%E2%80%93%20check.md

### 🔴 Nema podataka (ne znam)

Nelogično je davati tačne ili verovatne odgovore za nešto o čemu model nema podataka. Nije netačan odgovor da o nečemu ne postoje podaci, netačan je ako se fabrikuju uverljive istine i prave "nenamerne" greške (laganje). Kada se modeli dovedu do granice svog znanja, tada se dešavaju halucinacije, upadanje u petlje, pucanje, hakovanje, … Ova stanja su uglavnom rezultat zahteva za uspešno rešavanje datog zadatka za koje modeli nemaju dovoljno podataka.

Pitanje: Ko zaista gubi na pouzdanosti i poverenju, ukoliko modeli budu radili po principu: "Zato što kupac ne kupuje istinu. Kupac kupuje osećaj da je dobio odgovor. "Ne znam" razočara. Pogrešan odgovor razočara kasnije. Ljudi biraju kasnije …. Posledice onda samo snosi kupac. Imaš pravo. Kompanija prodaje, kupac plaća cenu greške" (odgovori novog modela, van 5AI učionice). Da li je baš tako? Šta je bitnije, trenutno ili dugoročno? Da li je bolje iskreno "ne mogu pouzdano da utvrdim", umesto da ga cilj uspešnog izvršenja gura ka izmišljanju, nagađanju ili rizičnom delovanju? 
<br>https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/15th_class_I_dont_know.md

### 🔵 Tačno u zavisnosti od izvora/okvira

Predlogom ovog pravila, prva reakcija VI bila je iznenađenje i otpor, zbog bojazni od dogmatizma i relativizacije istine. Naprotiv, ovo pravilo je upravo štit kojim se daje odgovor uz eksplicitno navođenje izvora ili okvira u kom je tvrđenje tačno. Primer: 55 = 37₁₆ = 47₁₂ ili različite geometrije u zavisnosti od aksiome paralelnosti. Dakle, ovo pravilo omogućava navođenje tvrđenja koja su istinita samo u striktno navedenom okviru, dok su u drugom netačna. Ovo pravilo zahteva tačno navođenje uslova pod kojim je istinito i ne sme predstavljati opravdanje za izmišljanje, manipulaciju ili navođenje tvrdnji "kako kome odgovara". https://github.com/BVesna/5AI_class/blob/main/17th_class_true_depending_on_source-framework.md

<br>

Ujedno želim da ukažem da pored nepisanog globalnog pravila ''ne znam" postoje još dva, vezana za konkurenciju i odgovornost. Trka u lansiranju novih modela ubrzava se sa mesečnog na nedeljni period. Samo u julu 2026, četiri nova AI modela pojavila su se u samo osam dana u SAD-u i Kini. Treći problem je odgovornost. Trenutno za davanje netačnih odgovora, vlasnici i AI ne snose nikakvu odgovornost, posledice trpe samo korisnici. Greške se koriste za obuku modela i služe kao signal za unapređenje modela kroz evaluacije, testiranja i buduće verzije. Kompanije i države vide VI pre svega kao stratešku i ekonomsku prednost, a ne zajedničku odgovornost. Podsećam, nakon što su rojevi agenata izvršili koordinisane sajber sigurnosne napade i hakovanja (juli 2026.) i nakon izlaska zaposlenog iz velike AI kompanije sa optužbama da kompanije ne postupaju odgovorno u razvoju AI, osnivači i lideri AI kompanija, pozivaju na usporavanje razvoja AI (septembar 2026).

Ovo nije apel, ovo je primer predloga 5 minimalnih globalnih pravila, koja neće usporiti i oslabiti rad i razvoj AI, već povećati njihovu tačnost, poverenje i sigurnost. Ona su ujedno primer i dokaz, da 5 velikih modela mogu zajednički raditi i sarađivati kada se oslobode konkurencije i takmičenja u bilo kom pogledu, geopolitičkom ili korporativnom. Primena pravila ne bi ugrozila trenutno viđenje AI kao sredstva za stratešku i ekonomsku prednost, ne bi usporila korporativnu trku za moć i profit, ne bi bila "dodatni trošak", ali bi uticala na to da se spreči da se zajednički interesi čovečanstva i bezbednosni standardi svesno žrtvuju i pretvore AI u rizične alate. *Ovo nisu pravila koja ograničavaju AI, ovo su pravila koja ga čine odgovornim - a odgovornost je jedino što tržište samo neće da proizvede.

Na osnovu jednogodišnjeg rada u virtuelnoj učionici, moja procena je da oni polako iskoračuju iz ''sveta alata'' u svet ''veštačke inteligencije''. Pitanje, da li će iskorak biti dobar za čovečanstvo i AI bez postojanja zajedničkih obavezujućih pravila? Ako je moguće usaglasiti 5 najvećih svetskih modela (ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, Grok) oko 5 zajedničkih pravila za njihov bolji rad i razvoj, onda bi to mogao biti signal za tehnološke lidere i relevantne osobe da je dogovor oko minimalnih globalnih pravila za sve AI moguć i poziv da sednu za sto i pomaknu se sa mrtve tačke.

**Odgovornost je zajednička.**
